# Changelog

Every release of Tether, newest first. Download any version from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/pranvirsingh/Tether/releases).

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-02

The first public release.

### New
- One-touch sling climbing: hold to tether, spin around the anchor, let go at the gold arrow for a perfect release
- Five zones (Dawnline, Nebula, Aurora, Eclipse, Singularity), each with its own hazards and twists
- Daily Climb: a run seeded from the date, with its own daily best
- Style shop with 6 orbs and 6 trails: the first of each is free, the rest are unlocked with motes
- Combos, near misses and a run summary after every climb
- Synthesised music and sound effects, with sound, music and vibration toggles
